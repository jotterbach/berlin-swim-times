    Notes

      • Entry and ticket sales close 60 minutes, swimming ends 30 minutes before closing time. “Open now” takes the entry cut-off into account.
      • Pools with several halls name the hall in the cell, e.g. Hallenbad and Traglufthalle (air dome, Seestraße) or Große and Kleine Halle (Neukölln).
      • Saunas and therapy pools are left out; a “Sommerpause” (summer break) only affects the sauna.
      • The most specific timetable wins: a one-off event day over school holidays over the season plan.
      • “no data”: the pool hasn't published a timetable for that day yet.
      • Same-day changes and cancellations are only on each pool's own page.
      • The pools publish their notices and session types in German only. We translated them as best we could; the German original is in parentheses, and it is what counts.

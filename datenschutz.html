Kurz gesagt

Diese Seite setzt keine Tracking-Cookies, nutzt keine Werbe- oder Tracking-Dienste und lädt keine externen Schriften. Eine einfache Zugriffsstatistik läuft ohne Cookies. 2. Auslieferung, Schutz und Statistik über Cloudflare

Die Seite wird über das Netzwerk von Cloudflare ausgeliefert (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). Jeder Aufruf läuft über Server von Cloudflare, die dabei technische Zugriffsdaten wie IP-Adresse, aufgerufene Seite, Browsertyp und Zeitpunkt verarbeiten, um die Seite schnell auszuliefern und vor Angriffen und Missbrauch zu schützen. Zum Schutz vor automatisierten Angriffen kann Cloudflare ein kurzlebiges Cookie (__cf_bm, Laufzeit 30 Minuten) setzen; es dient allein der Sicherheit und ist dafür unbedingt erforderlich (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG).

Für eine einfache Zugriffsstatistik nutzen wir Cloudflare Web Analytics. Dafür lädt die Seite ein kleines Skript von Cloudflare, das ohne Cookies und ohne Speicherung auf deinem Gerät auskommt und keine Profile über mehrere Seiten oder Besuche hinweg bildet. Erfasst werden zusammengefasste Werte wie aufgerufene Seiten, Herkunftsseite, Land, Gerätetyp und Ladezeit.

Rechtsgrundlage ist Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO (berechtigtes Interesse an einer sicheren, schnellen Seite und an einer datensparsamen Reichweitenmessung). Cloudflare verarbeitet die Daten als Auftragsverarbeiter; dabei können Daten in die USA übermittelt werden. Cloudflare ist unter dem EU-US Data Privacy Framework zertifiziert. Details: Datenschutzerklärung von Cloudflare.

3. Standortfreigabe im Browser

Der Knopf „Mein Standort“ auf der Karte fragt deinen Standort über die Standortfunktion deines Browsers ab. Das passiert nur, wenn du den Knopf drückst und der Abfrage im Browser zustimmst. Die Position wird nur als Punkt auf der Karte gezeigt und für die Luftlinie zu den Bädern verwendet; dafür verlässt sie deinen Browser nicht und wird nicht gespeichert.

4. Karte (OpenStreetMap, ÖPNVKarte)

Die Karte wird erst geladen, wenn du auf „Karte zeigen“ klickst. Dann lädt dein Browser die Kartenbilder von den Servern der OpenStreetMap Foundation (St John’s Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, Großbritannien). Schaltest du auf der Karte „ÖPNV“ ein, kommen die Kartenbilder stattdessen von der ÖPNVKarte (memomaps.de, Deutschland). Dabei werden technisch notwendige Zugriffsdaten wie deine IP-Adresse, der angezeigte Kartenausschnitt und der Browsertyp übertragen. Dein Standort und eine eingegebene Adresse werden dabei nicht übermittelt. Rechtsgrundlage ist Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO (berechtigtes Interesse an einer Kartendarstellung, die du selbst aufrufst). Für Großbritannien besteht ein Angemessenheitsbeschluss der EU-Kommission. Details: Datenschutzerklärung der OpenStreetMap Foundation, memomaps.de.

Die Wettervorhersage bei Freibädern laden wir einmal täglich selbst von Open-Meteo.com und liefern sie von dieser Seite aus; dein Browser fragt dafür keinen weiteren Dienst an.

Die Links „In Karten öffnen“ und „Route“ führen zu Google Maps, auf iPhone und iPad zu Apple Maps und auf Android-Geräten zur dort eingestellten Karten-App. Erst wenn du einen solchen Link anklickst, öffnet dein Gerät die Seite oder App des jeweiligen Anbieters, der dann eigene Daten nach seinen Datenschutzbestimmungen verarbeitet. Vorher wird nichts an Google oder Apple übertragen.

Details: Datenschutzhinweise der Stadtwerke München.

Privacy policy

In short

This site sets no tracking cookies, uses no advertising or tracking services and loads no external fonts. Simple visitor statistics run without cookies. Personal data is only processed when you open the site (technical access data at Cloudflare and the host) and when you open the map (map images from OpenStreetMap or ÖPNVKarte), and on the Munich, Frankfurt, Vienna and Zurich pages, which load current visitor numbers or the visitor light directly from Stadtwerke München, eaero, the City of Vienna or crowdmonitor.ch.

1. Hosting on GitHub Pages

The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). Because the site is delivered through Cloudflare (section 2), it is usually Cloudflare that fetches the files from GitHub, so GitHub mainly processes technical access data from Cloudflare. If you open the site directly at GitHub, GitHub processes technically necessary access data such as your IP address, date and time, the requested file and your browser type, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing the site securely and reliably). Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details: GitHub's privacy statement.

2. Delivery, protection and statistics via Cloudflare

The site is delivered through Cloudflare's network (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). Every request passes through Cloudflare's servers, which process technical access data such as IP address, requested page, browser type and time, to deliver the site quickly and protect it against attacks and abuse. To defend against automated attacks, Cloudflare may set a short-lived cookie (__cf_bm, 30 minutes). It serves security only and is strictly necessary for that purpose (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

For simple visitor statistics we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. The site loads a small Cloudflare script for this, which uses no cookies, stores nothing on your device and builds no profiles across pages or visits. It records aggregated values such as pages viewed, referring page, country, device type and load time.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure, fast site and in privacy-friendly audience measurement). Cloudflare processes the data on our behalf; data may be transferred to the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details: Cloudflare's privacy policy.

3. Location sharing in the browser

The “My location” button on the map asks for your position through your browser's location feature. This only happens when you press the button and agree to the browser's prompt. The position is only shown as a dot on the map and used to work out straight-line distances to the pools; it does not leave your browser and is not stored.

4. Map (OpenStreetMap, ÖPNVKarte)

The map only loads when you click “Show map”. Your browser then loads the map images from servers of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (St John’s Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom). If you switch on “Transit” on the map, the map images come from ÖPNVKarte instead (memomaps.de, Germany). This transfers technically necessary access data such as your IP address, the map area shown and your browser type. Your location and any address you entered are not sent. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a map you open yourself). The European Commission has issued an adequacy decision for the United Kingdom. Details: OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy, memomaps.de.

We fetch the weather forecast for outdoor pools from Open-Meteo.com ourselves once a day and serve it from this site; your browser does not contact any other service for it.

The “Open in Maps” and “Directions” links go to Google Maps, to Apple Maps on iPhone and iPad, and to the default map app on Android devices. Only when you click such a link does your device open that provider’s site or app, which then processes data under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent to Google or Apple before that.

5. Live occupancy (Munich, Frankfurt, Vienna, Zurich)

On the Munich page we show how busy the pools that are open right now are (“67% free”). Your browser loads these visitor numbers directly from Stadtwerke München (Stadtwerke München GmbH, Emmy-Noether-Straße 2, 80992 München, Germany) about every three minutes while the page is open and shows the current time. Stadtwerke München receive technically necessary access data such as your IP address, date and time and the browser type; no cookies, location or entered address are sent. The Berlin pages don't do this. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in up-to-date information on how busy a pool is). Details: Stadtwerke München privacy notice.

On the Frankfurt page we show the current state (green, orange, red) of pools that have a visitor light (currently Main Bad Bornheim). Your browser loads it directly from BäderBetriebe Frankfurt's booking system, eaero (morbitzer media GmbH, Lange Wisch 6, 27798 Hude-Wüsting, Germany), also only while the page shows the current time and about every three minutes. eaero receives technically necessary access data such as your IP address, date and time and the browser type; no cookies, location or entered address are sent. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Details: eaero privacy policy.

On the Vienna page we show, in summer, the City of Vienna's crowd forecast (Bäderampel) for today for the city's pools. Your browser loads it directly from the City of Vienna's open-data portal (data.wien.gv.at; Stadt Wien, Rathaus, 1082 Wien, Austria), only while the page shows the current time and about every three minutes; out of season not at all. The City of Vienna receives technically necessary access data such as your IP address, date and time and the browser type; no cookies, location or entered address are sent. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

On the Zurich page we show how busy the pools that are open right now are (“67% free”). Your browser loads these numbers directly from the visitor counter the City of Zurich uses for its pools (crowdmonitor.ch, Switzerland; Switzerland is covered by an EU adequacy decision) over a WebSocket connection, also only while the page shows the current time and about every three minutes. The operator receives technically necessary access data such as your IP address, date and time and the browser type; no cookies, location or entered address are sent. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

6. Storage in your browser (localStorage)

The site stores your language choice, your favourites, the chosen view (day or 7 days), and whether the transit map is switched on in your browser. This data does not leave your browser. You can delete it in your browser settings. The legal basis is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary for the service you asked for).

7. No tracking, no advertising, no external fonts

Apart from Cloudflare's security cookie (section 2), no cookies are set. There are no advertising or tracking services, and apart from Cloudflare's statistics script no scripts or fonts are loaded from third parties. The site uses your device's system fonts.

8. Storage periods and voluntary use

We do not store visitors' personal data ourselves. Data in your browser stays until you delete it. Technical logs at GitHub, Cloudflare, Stadtwerke München, eaero, the City of Vienna and crowdmonitor.ch are deleted according to these providers' retention periods, usually within days to weeks. Using the site and sharing your location are voluntary; without your location you still see all times, just without distances.

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, to data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 15–21 GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. To do so, write to berlin-swim-times@johannesotterbach.com.
